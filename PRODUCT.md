# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS in a single `index.html`, Google Fonts allowed, no heavy dependencies (pinned by the brief).

## Users
Prospective personal-training clients in Recife – PE and online: adults who want to train with a plan (hypertrophy, weight loss, conditioning) at home or at the gym, and who decide by chatting on WhatsApp. Secondary audience right now: Ricardo himself, evaluating this page as a pitch template.

## Product Purpose
One-page site for personal trainer Ricardo Fragoso that turns a visitor into a WhatsApp conversation. Success = taps on "Agendar consulta" / WhatsApp links.

## Positioning
Personalised, sustainable training ("treino inteligente", strategy, discipline, consistency) offered presencial, online consultancy and at-home, with initial assessment and continuous adjustment — not ready-made workouts.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Every CTA opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message (placeholder number 5581900000000).
- Prices and schedules are handled on WhatsApp, never on the page.
- Phone, Instagram (@ricardofragoso.personal), CREF and e-mail are placeholders until confirmed.

## Brand Commitments
Clean, minimal, sophisticated, masculine; generous whitespace. Palette pinned: dark moss/near-black ground (#141A16 / #1B221D), sand off-white sections (#F2EFE6), sage/olive accent (#8A9A6E), muted mustard highlight (#D8C27A) used sparingly. No pink, delicate or feminine tones. Condensed/geometric display sans + Inter body.

## Evidence on Hand
None yet. All photos are placeholders; testimonials, numbers, bio and certifications are labeled sample copy (inferred from the brief, to be replaced). Do not present them as real.

## Product Principles
1. WhatsApp is the only conversion; it is never more than a thumb away.
2. Personal over generic: every message says "plan made for you".
3. Calm confidence: no hype, no shouting, no fake urgency.
4. Easy to hand over: copy, colors and photos must be trivially editable.
